<h3> * Description</h3>
  <h5>Client </h5>
 <p>Customers visit the book-selling website to browse or search for books, log in or register, update personal details such as name and avatar, and manage their cart. They can adjust quantities, remove items, and choose "Pay on Delivery" to complete their purchase.</p>
   <h5>Admin </h5>
 <p>Admins can manage user accounts, product listings, categories, and orders. They have the ability to show or hide categories, add products with details such as name, price, and images, and update website settings including the logo and contact information.</p>
